How to play

Skip stones across a quiet lake with a basket of 10. Score skips, distance and bonuses. Swipe up to throw: speed is power, a shorter flick throws lower, and turning the phone or angling the swipe aims.

Ring, Hole or Gate+20 to +100
Three Hoops in a line+80 to +120 each, +100
Creature behind the target: Ball, Jetski, Croc, Dolphin, Loch Ness, Grandma+100 to +300
Far creature, 60 m or more+50% bonus
Clean sweep: target and creature+100 and a stone
15 skips, or 100 m+150 and a stone each
Back-to-back bonuses: comboup to x2

Daily Challenge: same targets and wind for everyone each day. Play once and keep your streak.
