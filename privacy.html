Neon Skip: Stone Skipping

Privacy policy. Last updated 1 November 2026.

This policy explains what information the Neon Skip mobile game ("the app") handles, why, and the choices you have. The app is published by SanSid Media, Bengaluru, Karnataka, India ("we", "us").

Summary

  • The app has no accounts and never asks for your name, email address, phone number or contacts.
  • Your game progress, coins, stars and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob and measures usage through Google Analytics for Firebase. These Google services collect device identifiers and usage data as described below.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details.
  • We do not sell your personal information. The app offers no cash prizes or real-money gaming.

Information stored on your device

Level progress, scores, stars, coin balance, stone upgrades, owned items and sound and haptics settings are saved in the app's local storage on your device. This information is not sent to us. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data. Items bought through Google Play can be restored from your Google account.

Consent and your choices

  • Ad consent. Where required by law, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent message before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time from Shop, then Privacy and ad choices, when that option is shown.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings, then Privacy, then Ads, on most devices). This limits ad personalisation.
  • Rewarded ads are optional. Ads that give extra stones or double coins play only when you choose them.
  • Remove Ads. A one-time purchase turns off interstitial ads.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all information stored on your device.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play under Google's terms. The app contains no random or loot-box purchases, and every item shows exactly what it contains before you buy.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and is listed for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app and information about them has been collected, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data retention

Information on your device stays until you uninstall the app or clear its data. Analytics event data is kept by Google Analytics for Firebase for the retention period we set, which is no longer than 14 months. Advertising data is retained by Google under its own policies.

Security

Information sent by the app to Google's services is encrypted in transit.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation and US state privacy laws, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, to withdraw consent and to raise a complaint with a regulator. Because the app has no accounts, we hold no information that identifies you by name. To make a request, contact us using the details below; we will respond within the time required by applicable law.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's store listing.
